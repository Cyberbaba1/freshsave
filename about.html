About FreshSave

Award-worthy projects usually combine clear purpose, polished design, and working features. FreshSave is built around all three.

The Problem

Many people throw away food because they forget what they bought or do not know how to use it in time.

The Solution

FreshSave makes expiry tracking simple, suggests recipe ideas, and shows measurable impact in one experience.

The Edge

This is not just a concept page. It works across multiple pages and stores data locally.

How it works

1

Add ingredients

Users enter food names, expiry dates, and categories on the tracker page.

2

Prioritize urgent foods

The site labels items as Fresh, Soon, Use Now, or Expired.

3

Match recipes

Recipe suggestions are generated from the actual ingredients being tracked.

4

Measure impact

Users see the estimated waste prevented, money saved, and meals rescued.
